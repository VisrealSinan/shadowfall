# Shadowfall v85 art and gameplay update

Graveshade is an original gravekeeper with bronze antlers, black funerary armour, a spirit lantern and spectral crescent sickle. The historical `myers` save ID is retained for compatibility.

All 23 classes and all three Druid styles offer 19 combat skills. Form selection skills are separate. Existing skill IDs and the six previously added skills per class/form remain intact.

Bone Colossus now receives reserved army slots before extra small summons. Dead summons retain their respawn slots. The cap remains 12 (plus earned talent/evolution bonuses).

Animation coverage: 328 existing full action-sheet variants are preserved, 32 secret-class race/gender variants receive live rigs, and 32 Druid form/race/gender variants receive live rigs. All support idle, run, attack, casting and dodge. The new rigs animate generated cutouts; they are not claimed to be hand-drawn frame sheets. Eight race-specific Cat and Tree designs are shared across genders, with distinct rig cadence. Summons and vendors also receive live movement rigs.

## Generated asset provenance and reproducible prompt set

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. PNG source pixels are preserved; `tools/build-v85-art.cjs` records atlas crop regions. Runtime drawing applies cropping, scale, palette and movement rigs without modifying source images. The prompts below are the exact generation and correction prompts used.
