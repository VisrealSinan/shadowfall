# Shadowfall v93 — The Hollow Bell

An optional mystery in normal Gloomwood hunts. At 5:00, a lost shrine appears nearby. Approach it and remain within 52 world units for three active seconds to awaken it. Leaving resets only the current channel. Follow spectral footprints and direction markers to two further shrines. The Unheard stalks the ritual; nearby enemies drift toward the active shrine.

The third shrine summons the Hollow Bellkeeper, an additional mortal encounter independent of the map's regular bosses. Its ground toll marks the player's position for 1.5 seconds before striking; move outside the ring to evade. Below half health, tolls occur more frequently.

Defeat grants Bellbound Vestments for every calling and a discovered Codex entry. Outfit palettes use existing class silhouettes and recoloring. Shrines and bell enemies use procedural canvas artwork. The ritual may be repeated; cosmetic ownership remains permanent.

Encounter positions, shrine progress, channel, boss health and pending toll persist with Save/Continue. Town, pause, death and Daily Challenges cannot advance it. Fresh hunts reset it.
